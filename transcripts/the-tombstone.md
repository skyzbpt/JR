And I see all these graves. These were people that sat next to us. They had family
dinners. They did things, but they didn’t accomplish their goals. They didn’t have a
way to change other people’s lives, so when they die, their mundane life died with
them. And everybody forgets them, even those that love them. Because when
Monday morning comes, everybody goes back to work. You may have been at their
funeral because you want to make sure that other people go to your funeral. But
who cares about the funeral anyway? It’s over. You get this? So I go and try to raise
the dead because it’s so unfair that they live their life and didn’t get what they
wanted.


So I’m saying, Lord, this is a good one. Joe Nobody. Wow. I don’t even know who he
is. Nobody knows anymore. But Joe, I just met you. I’m going to tell you something.
I found something where we can live, man. We can do anything that we want. We
can help other people, we can give our kids the education they want, we can go on
cruises, we can have fun. We can do important things that we wanted to create.
Man, I heard that you like to fly airplanes. We can make an airplane. We can do
anything that we want that makes us feel fulfilled and add to the world. Come on,
man, get up! We can have it all! No response. He doesn’t get up. It’s too late now.


There are two dates on there. There’s a birth date and a death date. It’s funny that
we had very little to do with either one. When you were born, you had nothing to do
with the date. You protested the whole way. You didn’t want to be here.


No teeth. You were fighting it. So was Mama. But you protested, it had nothing to
do with it. Then you got used to it. And then life goes by, those dreams don’t
happen, you get stuck in a rut and that day comes your number’s called. Nobody
gets out of life alive. I haven’t met anyone yet, right? So that number will come up.
It’s inevitable. Doesn’t matter when it comes up, it’s how much you lived and it’s how
much you helped other people and made the world a better place and made your
life count and enjoyed it because you only got one this incarnation. There you go.


And then there’s a death date that we had nothing to do with. We didn’t pick it. Your
number came up, you still have a to-do list, stuff to do. And we protest that too. We
weren’t ready to come in with the first date, and we don’t want to go out on the
second date. I got stuff to do! I promised my grandkids this and that! It’s not fair!
You got a good friend Ralph. Ralph’s the guy that told you nothing would work.
